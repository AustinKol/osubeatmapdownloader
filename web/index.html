osu! Beatmap Downloader

Bulk-download beatmaps from community mirrors, then import them into osu! in one click.

No osu! account needed. Beatmaps come from community mirrors, several at once, so there's no download limit to wait out. Maps no mirror has can be fetched from osu.ppy.sh afterwards, if you want them.

Is this safe?
You sign in on osu.ppy.sh in a normal Chrome window, including any captcha or email code osu! asks for. The app doesn't control that window or read what you type. When you're done it closes the window and checks with osu! that you're signed in.

Your sign-in is kept in this app's data folder (it lasts about a month), so don't share that folder. Sign out removes it. Google Chrome must be installed for this step only.

Sign in to osu! in the Chrome window that just opened (including the captcha and any email code). When you're signed in, click the button below, or just close that window.

Can't see it? Check your taskbar for a new Chrome window.
osu! allows about 200 downloads per hour (more with osu!supporter). The app waits and retries automatically after 5, 10, 20 and 25 minutes, so this step can take a while. Nothing is skipped.
