osu! Beatmap Downloader

Bulk-download beatmaps in the background, then import them into osu! in one click.

1 Connect your osu! account

osu! only lets signed-in players download maps. Paste your session cookie so the downloader can act as you.

Where do I find my cookie?
  1. Sign in at osu.ppy.sh in your normal browser.
  2. Press F12 to open developer tools.
  3. Chrome / Edge: Application tab → Cookies → https://osu.ppy.sh.
    Firefox: Storage tab → Cookies → https://osu.ppy.sh.
  4. Find the row named osu_session and copy its Value.
⚠ Treat this like a password — anyone with it can use your account. Signing out of osu! in your browser invalidates it.

You're connected. The downloader signs in with your session — no need to close Chrome.

Import into i osu!lazer stores beatmaps as files named by their SHA-256 hash, so you won't see normal song folders you can browse, back up or share.

Importing into osu!stable keeps a regular Songs folder — and you can still use those maps in lazer by pointing it at your stable install (lazer → Settings → Maintenance → import from stable).
Missing some maps? Turn on Show explicit content in your osu! account settings — otherwise osu! hides the download button for those maps.
