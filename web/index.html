Is this safe?
You sign in on osu.ppy.sh in a normal Chrome window, including any captcha or email code osu! asks for. The app doesn't control that window or read what you type. When you're done it closes the window and checks with osu! that you're signed in.

Your sign-in is kept in this app's data folder (it lasts about a month), so don't share that folder. Sign out removes it. The app is open source.

Sign in to osu! in the Chrome window that just opened (including the captcha and any email code). When you're signed in, click the button below, or just close that window.

Export my library
Import into i osu!lazer stores beatmaps as files named by their SHA-256 hash, so you won't see normal song folders you can browse, back up or share.

Importing into osu!stable keeps a regular Songs folder, and you can still use those maps in lazer by pointing it at your stable install (lazer → Settings → Maintenance → import from stable).
Missing some maps? Turn on Show explicit content in your osu! account settings. Otherwise, osu! hides the download button for those maps.
